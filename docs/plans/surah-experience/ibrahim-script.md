# "Inside the Surah - Ibrahim" - Master Script (English)

**Status:** APPROVED (Gate 2), implemented in `Thaqalayn/Content/SurahIbrahimDive.swift`, and revised per the Stage 5 four-auditor audit (flow, sourcing/Arabic, readability, voice & reverence). The Swift file is the shipped source of truth; this doc mirrors its final copy at the sourcing/structure level (beat copy lives in the Swift file). English-first; UR/AR deferred.
**Surah:** 14 (Ibrahim), 52 verses, Meccan. Gating: PREMIUM (default). Placement: catalog entry at surahNumber 14 (between Yusuf and Yasin in the current catalog; between al-Ra'd and al-Hijr in the mushaf). Cover art wired: `IbrahimCover.imageset` (1170x1452 q78 3x JPEG, bundled from the tranche-1 master `assets/premium-art/covers/cover_ibrahim.png`), `coverAssetName: "IbrahimCover"` in the catalog.
**Framing:** The weight of a word. Movement I - the two promises: God's speech (the Book with work to do 14:1, the mother tongue 14:4, the oath-bound proclamation 14:7) against Satan's confessed counterfeit (14:22). Movement II - the two trees: the rooted word (14:24-25), the uprooted word (14:26), the firm word that answers in the grave (14:27), and the al-Kafi narration naming the tree part by part (root = the Prophet ﷺ, branch = Ali, boughs = the Imams, fruit = their knowledge, leaves = the believers). Movement III - the valley: Ibrahim's prayer as the parable's living proof, held spoiler-clean until the climax (2:129), where all four receipts land together and the surah's name is explained. A focused thematic slice (the Days of God panel 14:5-8, the messengers-vs-peoples dialogue 14:9-21, the ashes verse 14:18, the blessings enumeration 14:31-34, and the closing warnings 14:42-51 are deliberately off-slice; the closing now discloses the slice).
**Depth:** 19 beats, est. 13 min. Climax verse from OUTSIDE the surah (2:129) - same species as al-Fatiha's climax. Controlling metaphor: SEED/TREE (the surah's own image). Window and door language avoided (Yunus owns the window, Tawba the door). 14:7 deliberately NOT the spine (Shukr has its own dive); it appears as God's proclaimed promise.

## Sourcing notes (post-audit; ► marks corrections)
- **14:1** Darknesses plural, light singular - stated as "the commentators pause on the grammar" (classical observation; al-Mizan makes it at the parallel 2:257). The Book-and-its-carriers reading - Ahl al-Bayt narrations under 14:1 (Nur al-Thaqalayn). VERIFIED generically attributed.
- **14:4** Accessible means; wants to be understood - al-Mizan. "No one can say I could not have known" - Imam Ali (a). VERIFIED.
- **14:7** ta'adhdhana as formal proclamation; law of creation; increase includes understanding/faith/nearness - al-Mizan. ► The promise/threat asymmetry stated editorially from the grammar; the threat half no longer called "only a fact" (inna + lam carry their own emphasis) - now "turns instead to a statement about the punishment itself."
- **14:22** ► "The only long speech" corrected to "the only extended speech the Qur'an ever lets him address to human beings" (he has longer addresses to God: 7:12-18, 15:32-42, 38:75-83; 14:22 is the longest overall and the only extended one to humans). ► Free-will point: al-Mizan "finds here a clear indication" (his actual register; the "clearest proof" superlative removed, and "the signature was always yours" removed per his amr-bayn-al-amrayn caveat - now "it holds no power over the one it calls"). ► Abandonment-at-the-hour-of-need is AL-MIZAN's point (kinaya for the severed bond, vol. 12), NOT Tabrisi's (Majma gives only a lexical gloss of musrikh) - attribution corrected; the app's tafsir_14.json layer2 had conflated them.
- **14:24-25** Good word = tawhid at base; fruit at every season = constant benefit; bi-idhni rabbiha guards against pride (al-Baqir (a)) - al-Mizan + layer-4 narrations. VERIFIED. ► Translation renders far'uha as singular "its branch" to hold the tree-chain consistent with the narration.
- **14:26** ► Tabrisi's actual point: the uprooted tree has no thabat/baqa (the wind takes it) and its holder gains nothing lasting - now stated as such. The no-soil-in-reality reasoning is AL-MIZAN's (ard al-haqa'iq) - now attributed to him. "Effort poured into promoting it" (layer2's phrasing) dropped.
- **14:27** "In the Hereafter" = the grave - Tabrisi (Majma) following the narrations. ► The questions: FOUR - Lord, religion, prophet, Imam (al-Kafi 3:236; the Suwayd ibn Ghafala narration Tabrisi quotes has Lord/religion/prophet; the tidy Lord/prophet/Imam triad is not transmitted and was corrected). Generic "The Ahl al-Bayt taught" framing retained (no single Imam owns the four-question form).
- **Tree narration** - Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq (a), al-Kafi 1:428 h.80, Kitab al-Hujja (via Amr ibn Hurayth; in Nur al-Thaqalayn under 14:24; Bihar 24:143; graded sahih by Majlisi): Messenger = root, Amir al-Mu'minin = branch (far'), Imams from their progeny = boughs (aghsan), knowledge of the Imams = fruit, their believing Shia = leaves. VERIFIED VERBATIM. ► The continuation is a birth/death COUPLET and is now printed whole: "a believer is born, and a leaf opens; a believer dies, and a leaf falls" (the death-only half alone belongs to the Kamal al-Din variant, not al-Kafi). ► "Oldest of the Shia hadith collections" corrected to "earliest of the four canonical" (Basa'ir al-Darajat, Tafsir al-Qummi, Kitab Sulaym predate al-Kafi).
- **14:35** ► Two prayers (2:126 indefinite "a city" vs 14:35 definite "this city") is AL-MIZAN's inference (12:54, explicit); Tabrisi REJECTS it (repeated indefinite becomes definite, like misbah/al-misbah) - attribution corrected to al-Mizan alone, and the citation/cross-surah frame removed for spoiler discipline. Idol-fear humility - al-Mizan. Idols beyond stone - Imam al-Sadiq (a). ► "at eighty" (invented specific) → "in his old age".
- **14:37** Worship above livelihood - al-Mizan. Provision through trade routes - Tabrisi. af'ida/tahwi physicality - editorial from root senses. ► The "some hearts, not all" aside CUT (density + third consecutive lexical note; Auditor A and C independently suggested it). The hearts-incline reading moved entirely to the climax.
- **14:39-40** ► 14:39 now folded into beat 16's reflection (the receipt already in his hand; "Hearer of supplication" cited by verse number). muqim al-salah = keeps prayer standing - al-Mizan. Pray for your prayer - Imam al-Baqir (a). Asking for acceptance as etiquette - al-Mizan/Tabrisi. VERIFIED.
- **CLIMAX** 2:127-129 - Qur'an. Sanctuary honored pre-Islam - al-Mizan. ► ATTRIBUTIONS SWAPPED per audit: the hearts-incline point is IMAM ALI's (a) - al-Ihtijaj: "and the hearts of the people incline to us - and that is the prayer of Ibrahim" (the only narration that explicitly names it Ibrahim's prayer; also al-Sadiq in Basa'ir al-Darajat). The descendants-point is IMAM AL-BAQIR's (a) - Majma al-Bayan 6:84: "We are the remnant of that progeny... Ibrahim's prayer was for us in particular" (the previous Imam-Ali attribution was not locatable and came from layer4). "I am the prayer of my father Ibrahim" - the Prophet ﷺ, al-Amali of Shaykh al-Tusi (via Ibn Mas'ud; Shia recension continues "...the call ended at me and at my brother; none of us ever prostrated to an idol"; in-house parallel Rawdat al-Kafi: al-Baqir (a), "We, by Allah, are the prayer of Ibrahim") - KEEP; do not print a page number without checking a print copy. ► Love-in-the-chest is now conditional ("If you find love...") and the planted-word law softened to "never wasted" (no guarantee of visible growth).
- **14:52 / closing** ► Quoted translation corrected to "This is a message delivered to mankind" (balagh is not kalima; the word-play lives in the surrounding prose). Merit - Imam al-Sadiq (a), Thawab al-A'mal p. 97 (also Tafsir al-Ayyashi 2:222): Ibrahim + al-Hijr in a two-rak'ah prayer every Friday - no poverty, madness, or affliction (► "sudden" dropped; balwa carries no such qualifier; source now named in-line). ► Closing now discloses the slice ("This descent walked its spine... and more waits inside it").

## Acts (movements)
1. **Wa'd al-Haqq** (وَعْدُ الْحَقِّ) - "The Promise of Truth" (14:1, 14:4, 14:7, 14:22)
2. **Al-Shajaratan** (الشَّجَرَتَانِ) - "The Two Trees" (14:24, 14:26, 14:27; the tree narration)
3. **Al-Wadi** (الْوَادِي) - "The Valley" (14:35, 14:37, 14:40 with 14:39 folded in; climax 2:129)

## Beats (final copy lives in the Swift file)
1. OPEN - the riddle: fifty-two verses, the prophet arrives only near the end, doing nothing but speaking. "The surah that weighs words."
2. ORIENTATION - Mecca, a Prophet ﷺ armed only with words; the claim (words are the most durable things in the world); leaveWith split into three sentences ending on the name.
3. ACT I divider - everything begins with God speaking; hook to "the only other promiser."
4. VERSE 14:1 "Out of the Darknesses" - plural/singular; the Book travels through a person; the rope.
5. VERSE 14:4 "In Your Own Tongue" - the mother tongue; li-yubayyina lahum glossed after the list; Imam Ali's edge.
6. VERSE 14:7 "The Proclaimed Promise" - ta'adhdhana gloss-first; binding oath vs statement about the punishment; "He binds Himself by them" close (tease of beat 7 cut).
7. VERSE 14:22 "The Other Promiser" - the only extended speech addressed to human beings; he weighs the promises himself; al-Mizan on freedom and on the severed bond; the reader-sized call.
8. ACT II divider - why did one word hold? Every word is a seed; two kinds of tree.
9. VERSE 14:24 "The Living Word" - root/branch/fruit tests; tawhid at base; by its Lord's permission.
10. VERSE 14:26 "The Uprooted Word" - ijtuththat; Tabrisi (no steadiness) + al-Mizan (no soil in reality); the empire fell in one push; the gentle turn to our own rootless words.
11. VERSE 14:27 "The Word That Holds You" - it holds him up; the grave's four questions, phrased as questions; the mirror with Satan's desertion; the companion that speaks when you cannot.
12. NARRATION "The Tree Has a Name" - al-Kafi 1:428 part by part; the full birth/death leaf couplet; you among the leaves.
13. ACT III divider - connector carries the household; "The word has been named. Now the surah shows you where it was planted"; those he loves most left in the valley's shade.
14. VERSE 14:35 "Even the Friend of God" - al-Mizan's two prayers (no citation, no cross-surah frame); the farmer tending the field; shirk glossed; the idol-fear humility, in his old age.
15. VERSE 14:37 "Planted in Dead Ground" - lays it plainly before his Lord; li-yuqimu al-salah as hinge; fruits via the world's trade routes; af'ida/tahwi birds; the purpose beneath it all (gratitude, the grain of 14:7). ("Some hearts" aside cut.)
16. VERSE 14:40 "Accept My Prayer" - muqim al-salah unpacked in short sentences; pray for your prayer; rabbana wa-taqabbal du'a; 14:39 folded in (the answer already in his hands - "but about the valley... nothing"); "Was it accepted?"
17. CLIMAX "The Answered Valley" [2:129] - body = setup only (the House, Isma'il, the request for a messenger); reflection = the count (city / fruits / hearts with Imam Ali's al-Ihtijaj reading fused in, conditional love / messenger with "I am the prayer of my father Ibrahim" and al-Baqir's "remnant of that progeny") + the tree on the exact spot + the name + the reader's own seeds ("never wasted... may not be yours to watch") + "your ground is not more barren than his was."
18. REFLECTION PROMPT "What word will you plant?" - recap with "the oath God bound Himself to."
19. CLOSING - essence: 14:52 quoted as "a message delivered to mankind," balagh glossed, "Its root is firm. Its branch is in the sky."; line: Thawab al-A'mal merit (named), the slice disclosed, "read it as what it says it is: seed."
